FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

How do I report an issue?

Sign in, snap a photo, drop a pin on the map and submit. Your report goes straight to the municipal team.

Can I track my report?

Yes. Each report moves through New, Assigned, In Progress and Closed, and you can follow it from your dashboard.

Do I need an account?

You can continue with Google or use an email and password. It only takes a moment.
